Barbell Bicep Curls

Free_weights Beginner Arms Pull
Barbell bicep curls are the classic exercise for building bicep size and strength. This isolation movement targets the biceps brachii and brachialis muscles. Execution Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent Grip the barbell with hands shoulder-width apart, palms facing up Keep your elbows close to your sides Curl the bar toward your shoulders Squeeze your biceps at the top Lower the bar with control Key Points Keep your elbows pinned to your sides Don’t use momentum or swing the weight Focus on the mind-muscle connection Control the negative phase Common Mistakes Using excessive body momentum Not getting full range of motion Letting elbows drift forward Using too much weight Variations Dumbbell curls (unilateral focus) Hammer curls (targets brachialis) Preacher curls (isolates biceps) Cable curls (constant tension)
