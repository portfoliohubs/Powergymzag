The dumbbell fly is an isolation exercise that targets the chest muscles, particularly the outer chest for width and shape. It's excellent for chest definition and stretching the muscle fibers.

## Execution

1. Lie on a flat bench holding dumbbells above your chest
2. Keep a slight bend in your elbows throughout the movement
3. Lower the dumbbells out to your sides in a wide arc
4. Feel the stretch in your chest at the bottom
5. Bring the dumbbells back together by squeezing your chest

## Key Points

- Maintain constant tension on the chest muscles
- Keep elbows slightly bent, not locked
- Focus on the mind-muscle connection
- Control the movement, don't use momentum

## Common Mistakes

- Bending elbows too much (turns it into a press)
- Using too much weight and losing form
- Dropping the weights too quickly
- Not getting a good stretch at the bottom

## Variations

- Incline dumbbell fly (targets upper chest)
- Decline dumbbell fly (targets lower chest)
- Cable fly (constant tension throughout)
