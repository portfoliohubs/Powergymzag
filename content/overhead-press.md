The overhead press (also called military press) is the premier exercise for building shoulder strength and size. This compound movement targets all three heads of the deltoids.

## Execution

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, barbell at collarbone level
2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width
3. Keep your core tight and glutes squeezed
4. Press the bar directly overhead until arms are fully extended
5. Lower the bar back to collarbone level with control

## Key Points

- Keep your core tight throughout the movement
- Don't arch your lower back excessively
- Press the bar in a straight line
- Keep your head neutral, don't push it forward

## Common Mistakes

- Using leg drive (turns it into push press)
- Arching lower back excessively
- Not pressing directly overhead
- Flaring elbows too wide

## Variations

- Seated overhead press (more stable)
- Dumbbell overhead press (unilateral)
- Arnold press (rotation component)
- Push press (uses leg drive for heavier weight)
