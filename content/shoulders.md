# Shoulders Training Program

Well-developed shoulders create the appearance of width and strength. This comprehensive program targets all three heads of the deltoids for complete shoulder development.

## Muscle Groups Targeted

- **Anterior Deltoids**: Front shoulder muscles
- **Medial Deltoids**: Side shoulder muscles
- **Posterior Deltoids**: Rear shoulder muscles
- **Trapezius**: Upper traps

## Training Benefits

- Builds shoulder width and roundness
- Improves shoulder strength and stability
- Enhances upper body aesthetics
- Supports overhead pressing movements
- Reduces injury risk

## Equipment Types

This program includes exercises using:
- Free Weights (overhead press, lateral raises)
- Machines (shoulder press machine)
- Cables (cable lateral raises, face pulls)

Balance is key - train all three heads of the deltoids for optimal shoulder development.
