The barbell squat is the king of leg exercises, building overall lower body strength and muscle mass. This compound movement targets quads, glutes, hamstrings, and core.

## Execution

1. Position the barbell across your upper back (trapezius muscles)
2. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly pointed out
3. Keep your chest up and core tight
4. Lower your body by bending at knees and hips
5. Go as low as you can while maintaining form
6. Drive through your heels to return to standing

## Key Points

- Keep your chest up and back straight
- Push your knees outward as you descend
- Keep your weight on your heels
- Maintain core tension throughout

## Common Mistakes

- Leaning too far forward
- Letting knees collapse inward
- Not going low enough (parallel or below)
- Raising heels off the ground

## Benefits

- Builds overall leg strength and size
- Improves core stability
- Increases testosterone and growth hormone
- Improves athletic performance
- Strengthens bones and joints
