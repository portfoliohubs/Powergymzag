# Push Training Program

The push workout focuses on all pushing movements of the upper body, primarily targeting the chest, shoulders, and triceps. This type of training is essential for building upper body strength and muscle mass.

## Muscle Groups Targeted

- **Chest**: Primary muscle for pushing movements
- **Shoulders**: Anterior and medial deltoids
- **Triceps**: Arm extensors for pushing power

## Training Benefits

- Builds upper body strength
- Improves pushing power for daily activities
- Enhances chest and shoulder development
- Creates balanced upper body physique

## Equipment Types

This program includes exercises using:
- Free Weights (barbells, dumbbells)
- Machines (chest press, shoulder press)
- Cables (cable fly, tricep pushdowns)

Choose exercises based on your available equipment and experience level.
