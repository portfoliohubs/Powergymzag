# Legs Training Program

Leg day is crucial for building a balanced, strong physique. This comprehensive workout targets all major lower body muscle groups including quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, and calves.

## Muscle Groups Targeted

- **Quadriceps**: Front thigh muscles
- **Hamstrings**: Back thigh muscles  
- **Glutes**: Hip extensors
- **Calves**: Lower leg muscles

## Training Benefits

- Builds lower body strength and power
- Improves athletic performance
- Enhances metabolism and hormone production
- Creates balanced physique
- Strengthens bones and joints

## Equipment Types

This program includes exercises using:
- Free Weights (squats, deadlifts, lunges)
- Machines (leg press, leg curl, leg extension)
- Cables (cable pull-throughs, hip abductions)

Never skip leg day - strong legs support your entire body and improve overall fitness!
