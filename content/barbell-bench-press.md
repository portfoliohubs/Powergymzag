The barbell bench press is the king of upper body exercises for building chest strength and mass. This compound movement targets the chest, shoulders, and triceps simultaneously.

## Execution

1. Lie on a flat bench with your eyes under the bar
2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width
3. Unrack the bar and lower it to your mid-chest
4. Press the bar back up to the starting position
5. Keep your feet flat on the floor throughout

## Key Points

- Maintain a slight arch in your lower back
- Keep your shoulder blades retracted and depressed
- Control the descent and explode on the way up
- Don't bounce the bar off your chest

## Common Mistakes

- Flaring elbows too wide (should be about 45 degrees)
- Lifting your hips off the bench
- Using partial range of motion
- Bouncing the weight off your chest

## Progression

Start with light weight to master form, then gradually increase the weight. Focus on progressive overload for continuous gains.
