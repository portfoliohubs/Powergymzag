Planks are one of the most effective core exercises for building overall core strength and stability. This isometric movement targets the entire core musculature.

## Execution

1. Place your forearms on the ground, elbows under shoulders
2. Extend your legs back, resting on your toes
3. Create a straight line from head to heels
4. Engage your core and glutes
5. Hold the position for the desired time
6. Keep breathing throughout

## Key Points

- Keep your hips level, don't let them sag
- Maintain a neutral spine position
- Engage your core muscles throughout
- Don't hold your breath

## Common Mistakes

- Letting hips sag or rise too high
- Holding your breath
- Looking up or down (keep neutral neck)
- Not engaging the core properly

## Progressions

- Increase hold time gradually
- Add weight plates on your back
- Side planks (target obliques)
- Plank variations (mountain climbers, etc.)

## Benefits

- Builds core stability and strength
- Improves posture
- Reduces back pain
- Enhances athletic performance
- Requires no equipment
