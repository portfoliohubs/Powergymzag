# Pull Training Program

The pull workout targets all pulling movements of the upper body, primarily working the back muscles and biceps. This training is crucial for developing a strong, balanced upper body and improving posture.

## Muscle Groups Targeted

- **Back**: Latissimus dorsi, rhomboids, trapezius
- **Biceps**: Primary arm flexors
- **Forearms**: Grip strength development

## Training Benefits

- Builds back width and thickness
- Improves posture and reduces back pain
- Enhances pulling strength for daily activities
- Creates V-taper physique

## Equipment Types

This program includes exercises using:
- Free Weights (barbells, dumbbells)
- Machines (lat pulldown, seated row)
- Cables (cable pulldowns, face pulls)

Progressive overload is key to back development - focus on proper form and gradual weight increases.
