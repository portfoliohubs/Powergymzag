# Arms Training Program

Well-developed arms are a hallmark of physical fitness. This comprehensive program targets both biceps and triceps for balanced arm development.

## Muscle Groups Targeted

- **Biceps Brachii**: Primary arm flexors
- **Brachialis**: Upper arm muscle
- **Triceps Brachii**: Arm extensors (2/3 of upper arm mass)
- **Forearms**: Grip and wrist strength

## Training Benefits

- Builds arm size and strength
- Improves pulling and pushing power
- Enhances overall upper body aesthetics
- Supports daily functional movements
- Creates balanced physique

## Equipment Types

This program includes exercises using:
- Free Weights (curls, extensions)
- Machines (preacher curl, tricep pushdown)
- Cables (cable curls, cable pushdowns)

Remember that triceps make up 2/3 of upper arm mass - give them equal attention for optimal arm development.
