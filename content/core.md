# Core Training Program

A strong core is the foundation of all movement and essential for overall fitness, posture, and injury prevention. This program targets all major core muscles.

## Muscle Groups Targeted

- **Rectus Abdominis**: Front abdominal muscles
- **Obliques**: Side abdominal muscles
- **Transverse Abdominis**: Deep core stabilizers
- **Erector Spinae**: Lower back muscles

## Training Benefits

- Improves core strength and stability
- Enhances posture and reduces back pain
- Supports all other exercises and movements
- Improves athletic performance
- Reduces injury risk

## Equipment Types

This program includes exercises using:
- Free Weights (weighted crunches, Russian twists)
- Machines (ab crunch machine)
- Cables (cable crunches, wood chops)
- Bodyweight (planks, leg raises)

Core training should be performed regularly with focus on proper form and progressive overload.
