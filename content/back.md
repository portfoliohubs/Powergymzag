# Back Training Program

A strong, well-developed back is crucial for overall strength, posture, and aesthetics. This program targets all major back muscles for complete development.

## Muscle Groups Targeted

- **Latissimus Dorsi**: Primary back muscles for width
- **Rhomboids**: Upper back muscles
- **Trapezius**: Upper and mid-back muscles
- **Erector Spinae**: Lower back muscles

## Training Benefits

- Builds back width and thickness
- Improves posture and reduces back pain
- Enhances pulling strength
- Creates V-taper physique
- Supports shoulder health

## Equipment Types

This program includes exercises using:
- Free Weights (deadlifts, bent-over rows)
- Machines (lat pulldown, seated row)
- Cables (cable pulldowns, face pulls)

Focus on mind-muscle connection and proper form for optimal back development.
