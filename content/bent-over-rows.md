Bent-over rows are a fundamental exercise for building back thickness and strength. This compound movement targets the lats, rhomboids, and traps while also engaging the biceps.

## Execution

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent
2. Hinge at your hips, keeping your back straight
3. Grip the barbell with an overhand grip, hands slightly wider than shoulders
4. Pull the bar toward your lower chest
5. Squeeze your back muscles at the top
6. Lower the bar with control

## Key Points

- Keep your back straight throughout the movement
- Pull with your back, not your arms
- Keep your elbows close to your body
- Don't round your lower back

## Common Mistakes

- Using too much weight and compromising form
- Rounding the back instead of hinging at hips
- Using momentum to lift the weight
- Not getting a full range of motion

## Variations

- Dumbbell rows (unilateral focus)
- Underhand grip rows (more bicep involvement)
- T-bar rows (different angle)
- Machine rows (guided movement)
