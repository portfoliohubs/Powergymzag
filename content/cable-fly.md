The cable fly provides constant tension throughout the entire range of motion, making it superior to dumbbell flies for muscle isolation and mind-muscle connection.

## Execution

1. Set pulleys at chest height on a cable machine
2. Grab the handles with palms facing forward
3. Step forward and create tension on the cables
4. Bring your hands together in front of your chest
5. Squeeze your chest muscles at the peak contraction
6. Slowly return to the starting position

## Key Points

- Keep a slight bend in your elbows throughout
- Focus on squeezing the chest muscles
- Control the negative phase (returning movement)
- Don't let the weights stack between reps

## Common Mistakes

- Using too much body momentum
- Locking elbows straight
- Not getting a full stretch at the start
- Rushing through the movement

## Variations

- High cable fly (targets lower chest)
- Low cable fly (targets upper chest)
- Single-arm cable fly (unilateral focus)
