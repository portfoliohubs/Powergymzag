Pull-ups are the ultimate upper body exercise for building back width and strength. This compound movement targets the lats, biceps, and upper back muscles.

## Execution

1. Grab the pull-up bar with an overhand grip, slightly wider than shoulder-width
2. Hang from the bar with your arms fully extended
3. Pull your body up until your chin passes the bar
4. Lower yourself back to the starting position with control
5. Keep your core tight throughout the movement

## Key Points

- Initiate the movement with your back muscles
- Keep your chest up and shoulders back
- Don't use excessive kipping or momentum
- Focus on full range of motion

## Common Mistakes

- Not getting your chin over the bar
- Using momentum instead of strength
- Shrugging shoulders up to ears
- Not controlling the negative phase

## Progressions

- Assisted pull-ups (machine or band)
- Negative pull-ups (focus on slow descent)
- Chin-ups (underhand grip)
- Weighted pull-ups (for advanced lifters)

## Benefits

- Builds impressive back width
- Develops functional upper body strength
- Improves grip strength
- Requires no equipment beyond a pull-up bar
